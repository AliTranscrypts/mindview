🔒 Privacy Policy

MindView Academy ("MindView", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of every student, parent, teacher, and administrator who uses this platform. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who can access it, and your rights under Canadian law.

Summary for students and parents: We collect your name, email address, and academic progress only to run your online courses. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising. You may request access to or deletion of your data at any time by emailing enrolment@mindviewacademy.ca.

1. Who We Are

MindView Virtual Classroom is an Ontario-based online learning platform delivering Grade 10–12 courses aligned with the Ontario Ministry of Education curriculum. Our platform is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (servers located in North America).

For privacy inquiries, contact the school administrator at: enrolment@mindviewacademy.ca

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver the course service — display lessons, assessments, and progress tracking to the correct user.
  • Teacher oversight — teachers can view the progress records of students enrolled in their courses so they can provide feedback and unlock assessments.
  • Account administration — administrators manage account creation, role assignment, and course enrolment.
  • Security — detect and prevent unauthorised access to student content.
  • Communication — contact you about your courses, assessment sessions, or account changes (email only).

We do not use your personal information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to delivering the educational service.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Account data is stored in Cloudflare Workers KV — a distributed key-value store with at-rest encryption, physically located in North America.
  • Session cookies are signed with HMAC-SHA256 and set as HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax — they cannot be read by JavaScript and are transmitted only over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes; we cannot recover your plain-text password.
  • All traffic between your browser and our platform is encrypted with TLS (HTTPS). Plain-HTTP connections are rejected.
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorised administrators.

6. Cookies

MindView uses a single first-party session cookie named mv_session. It is set when you log in and cleared when you log out or close your browser session. It contains no personal information in readable form — only a cryptographic signature that lets the server verify you are who you say you are.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies.

YouTube videos embedded in course pages are served via YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed (youtube-nocookie.com). Under this mode, YouTube does not set tracking cookies unless you interact with the video player. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

7. Children and Minors (PIPEDA / Provincial Requirements)

Our courses are designed for students aged 14–18 (Ontario secondary school Grades 10–12). We recognise that some students may be under 18.

  • Student accounts are created by a school administrator, not by the student themselves, ensuring an adult-mediated enrolment process.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without verified parental consent.
  • Parents or guardians of students under 18 may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's records by contacting us.

8. Data Retention

  • Active accounts — retained for the duration of enrolment.
  • Progress records — retained for one academic year after the end of enrolment, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Session cookies — expire when you close your browser or when the server-side session is invalidated at logout.
  • Cloudflare request logs — retained per Cloudflare's standard log-retention schedule (typically 30 days); see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

9. Your Rights Under PIPEDA

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Withdrawal of consent — withdraw consent for non-essential uses (note: withdrawing consent for core educational uses will require account closure).
  • Complaint — lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at enrolment@mindviewacademy.ca. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Email Communications (CASL)

Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL, S.C. 2010, c. 23) governs commercial electronic messages (CEMs).

  • Transactional emails only: MindView sends email solely for transactional purposes — account creation confirmations, assessment session scheduling, course access notifications, and replies to your inquiries. These are not commercial messages and are exempt from CASL's express-consent requirement.
  • No marketing emails: We do not send newsletters, promotional emails, or any commercial electronic messages. We will not send marketing email to you without your express, written consent.
  • Unsubscribe: If you receive any email from us that you believe is commercial in nature, you may reply with "UNSUBSCRIBE" in the subject line and we will stop all non-transactional email immediately. We will always identify ourselves as MindView Virtual Classroom and provide our contact information in every email.
