💳 Refund & Cancellation Policy

MindView Virtual Classroom ("MindView") is an Ontario-registered private school. This policy outlines your cancellation rights and our refund procedures, in compliance with the Ontario Consumer Protection Act, 2002 (S.O. 2002, c. 30).

Summary: You have a 7-day cooling-off period after enrollment confirmation. If you cancel within 7 days and have not accessed any course material, you receive a full refund — no questions asked. After accessing materials, partial or no refund applies depending on how far you progressed.

1. Your 7-Day Cancellation Right

Under the Ontario Consumer Protection Act, 2002, s. 43, you have the right to cancel an internet agreement within 7 calendar days of receiving your enrollment confirmation, without giving any reason and at no cost to you, provided you have not yet accessed any course materials (i.e., your account has not been activated or you have not logged in to view any lesson content).

  • To exercise this right, email enrolment@mindviewacademy.ca within 7 days of your enrollment confirmation email.
  • Your refund will be processed within 15 business days of your cancellation notice.
  • Refunds are returned via the same method as the original payment.
Note: Once you have logged in and accessed any lesson content, video, or assessment material, the 7-day free cancellation is no longer available. The pro-rated refund schedule in Section 2 applies instead.

2. Refund Schedule After Course Access

Once course materials have been accessed, refund eligibility is determined by how much of the course you have completed (measured as a percentage of chapters/assessments unlocked):

3. How to Request a Refund

1
Email your refund request Send an email to enrolment@mindviewacademy.ca with the subject line: "Refund Request — [your full name] — [course code]"
2
Include in your email:
  • Your full name and email address used for enrollment
  • The course(s) you wish to cancel
  • Your enrollment confirmation date
  • The reason for cancellation (optional but helpful for improving our service)
3
Receive a confirmation within 2 business days We will confirm receipt of your request, advise on the applicable refund amount, and close your course access.
4
Refund processed within 15 business days Refunds are returned via the same payment method. If the original payment method is unavailable, we will contact you to arrange an alternative.

4. HST on Refunds

Where a refund of tuition is issued, the corresponding portion of HST paid will also be refunded.

5. Complaints

If you are dissatisfied with a refund decision, you may:

  • Contact the school administrator at enrolment@mindviewacademy.ca to escalate your concern.
  • File a complaint with Consumer Protection Ontario (1-800-889-9768) if you believe your rights under the Consumer Protection Act have not been respected.

6. Contact

Email: enrolment@mindviewacademy.ca
Response time: within 2 business days for refund matters
