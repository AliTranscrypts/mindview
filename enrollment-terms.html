MindView Virtual Classroom is an Ontario-registered private school operating under the Ontario Education Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. E.2. Students who successfully complete a course may be eligible to receive credit toward their Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD), subject to successful completion as determined by the school's qualified teachers.
