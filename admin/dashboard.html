📋 Supervised Assessment Requirements

FOR Diagnostics, OF Unit Tests, and Final Exams are server-locked. Students see a "Request Access" button instead of the exam paper. When a student requests access, their request appears in the Authorization Centre above. You may only grant access when all four of the following conditions are true — the authorization checklist enforces this.

🟢
Both online simultaneously

You and the student must both be online and actively present at the same time before and during the exam.

📷
Student visible via camera

You must be able to see the student clearly via Jitsi video. They must be alone, at their desk, throughout the exam.

🖨️
Working printer confirmed

The student must have a working printer with paper loaded and ready — shown on camera or confirmed verbally before you unlock.

📵
No external aids visible

No textbooks, notes, phones, or other devices visible. The student's desk and workspace must be clear of all resources.

Your access

As a student, you have full access to all enrolled course materials, video lessons, and Practice Quiz (AS) assessments.

FOR Diagnostics, OF Unit Tests, and Final Exams are supervised — your teacher must be online with you via video call to authorize access. Click the "Request Access" button on any locked assessment to notify your teacher.
